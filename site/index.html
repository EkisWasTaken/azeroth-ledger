Windows: get the Ledger apprecommended

One download, then just play.

Download for Windows
  1. Download the app and run it once. It installs itself and the FunStats addon in every WoW folder it finds.
  2. It starts with Windows and waits quietly in the tray.
  3. Every time you log out of WoW, your stats appear here.
  4. It updates itself: new versions of the app and the addon arrive on their own. Restart WoW to load a new addon.

If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. The app is small and only uploads your FunStats file.

If WoW lists FunStats as out of date, tick Load out of date AddOns on the character screen. It works fine; that just means a WoW patch came out before our next update.

Already have it? Nothing to do, it keeps itself up to date. Only apps from before version 1.1 need one more download.

To stop it: right-click the shield icon in the tray and untick Start with Windows, or choose Quit.

Mac, or without the app
  1. Download FunStats.zip and put the FunStats folder in World of Warcraft\_anniversary_\Interface\AddOns.
  2. Play, then log out so WoW saves your stats.
  3. Drop WTF\Account\YOUR_ACCOUNT\SavedVariables\FunStats.lua here:

Without the app, the addon doesn't update itself: download FunStats.zip again now and then, and upload your file after you play.

Guild mates without the addon still show up: with their level and class from the guild roster, and their gear from the Blizzard armory.

In gamenothing to type

FunStats records on its own. It shares stats with group and guild mates who have it, and also tracks guild mates and WoW friends who don't.

/funstats
Today's numbers and these options
/funstats bosses
Your latest boss kills and the dungeon run you're in
/funstats sync
Whose stats your addon has received
/funstats announce
Post your deaths in group chat (off until you turn it on)
/funstats add Name
Also track someone outside your guild and friends list; friends lists them, remove Name stops
