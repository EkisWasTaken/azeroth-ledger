Legendary Pixel Icons

32×32 icons for Azeroth Ledger, inspired by legendary and memorable Classic gear. All are original pixel art with their own names, drawn in the site's existing sprite format so they drop straight into SPRITES. The border colour is the item's rarity.

Sizes

32×32 should be shown at whole multiples (32, 64 or 96 px) to stay crisp. The current stat tiles show 8×8 sprites at 16 px, so they'd grow to 32 px.
