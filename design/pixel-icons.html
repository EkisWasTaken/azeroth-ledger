Legendary Pixel Icons

16×16 icons for Azeroth Ledger, inspired by legendary and memorable Classic gear. All are original pixel art with their own names, drawn in the site's existing sprite format so they drop straight into SPRITES. The border colour is the item's rarity.

Sizes

16×16 should be shown at whole multiples (16, 32 or 64 px) to stay crisp. The site uses 16 px next to text, 32 px in tiles and cards, and 64 px for shields and portraits.
